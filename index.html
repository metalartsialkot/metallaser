Premium Islamic Metal Wall Art - Handcrafted in Sialkot, Pakistan

Welcome to Metal Art Sialkot, your premier destination for exquisite Islamic wall art. We specialize in handcrafted stainless steel Islamic calligraphy featuring verses from the Holy Quran. Each piece is meticulously crafted by skilled artisans in Sialkot, Pakistan, using premium 0.8mm stainless steel with a luxurious golden mirror finish.

Why Choose Metal Art Sialkot?

  • Premium Quality Materials: We use only high-grade 0.8mm stainless steel that ensures durability and a brilliant golden shine.
  • Handcrafted Excellence: Every piece is carefully laser-cut and hand-finished by experienced artisans in Sialkot.
  • 5-Year Rust-Free Guarantee: Our products come with a comprehensive 5-year guarantee against rust and fading.
  • Free Shipping Across Pakistan: We deliver to all major cities including Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, and beyond.
  • Custom Sizes Available: Need a specific size? We offer custom dimensions to fit your wall perfectly.

Our Islamic Calligraphy Collection

Our collection includes Ayat ul Kursi wall art, Masha Allah calligraphy, Bismillah wall hanging, and various Kalima designs. Each piece serves as both a spiritual reminder and an elegant decorative element for your home, office, or mosque.

Perfect Islamic Gifts

Looking for unique Islamic gifts? Our metal wall art makes perfect presents for weddings, housewarmings, Ramadan, Eid celebrations, or any special occasion. Each piece comes carefully packaged and ready to display.
